# Cosmos Author Courses

Write missions for Artemis Cosmos, the starship
bridge simulator. These courses are for people who tell stories, not people who program.
You do not need to know markdown, a command prompt or a code editor before you start. The
first class teaches all three.

!!! warning "This is a review copy"
    Classes 1, 2 and 3 are complete as drafts and are being read and tried by reviewers.
    Pages and videos will change. If you are one of the reviewers, start with
    [For reviewers](reviewers.md).

Every video so far is in one [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAW6YCoCOmSk),
in order.

## Class 1 - The Writer's Desk and Your First Quest

Twelve lectures. Each is a page you follow at your own pace and a short video (four to
ten minutes) that shows it being done. You finish with a mission of your own: a quest with
several steps, a place to find, a win and a loss, checked, printed and handed to a friend.

All twelve videos are also in one [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLbwJW0GVtYR8).

## Class 2 - People, Dialogue and Siege Bosses

Eleven lectures. Your mission gets sides that are friends and enemies, people with names
and faces, calls the crew answers on Comms, and a side that remembers what the crew did.
Then you write a boss for the Siege game: a flagship with her own fleets, her own quests,
and a voice.

## Class 3 - Boarding Parties

Sixteen lectures. Your crew go aboard as themselves. First a scene of rooms: a derelict
hulk where each person is offered different choices, where skills decide who can do what,
and where two of the crew carry a story of their own. Then the party walks: maps you draw,
things to pick up and use, people to talk to, something that fights, a clock, and an enemy
ship boarded after she strikes her colors. You finish with a long away mission of your own.

All sixteen videos are also in one [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDtp68Wa9ogg).

## What you need

- Artemis Cosmos 1.4.0, from Steam or itch.io.
- A Windows computer you can install a free editor on.
- About an hour a lecture, most of it your own typing.
