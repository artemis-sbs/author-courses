# Cosmos Author Courses

Write missions for Artemis Cosmos, the starship
bridge simulator. These courses are for people who tell stories, not people who program.
You do not need to know markdown, a command prompt or a code editor before you start. The
first class teaches all three.

!!! warning "This is a review copy"
    Classes 1 to 6 are complete as drafts and are being read and tried by reviewers.
    Pages and videos will change. If you are one of the reviewers, start with
    [For reviewers](reviewers.md).

Every video so far is in one [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAW6YCoCOmSk),
in order.

ten minutes) that shows it being done. You finish with a mission of your own: a quest with
several steps, a place to find, a win and a loss, checked, printed and handed to a friend.

things to pick up and use, people to talk to, something that fights, a clock, and an enemy

## Class 4 - Ancient Ruins and EVA

Ten lectures. A ruin is a place in space a ship can fly inside: rooms, passages and a haze,
older than anyone who could have built it. You build one room by room, dress it, give its
places something to say, hide things in it, and give it a story the crew follows through it.
Then the crew go outside in suits. You finish with one whole ruin episode of your own.

## Class 6 - Open Universe: Running the Campaign

Eleven lectures. A campaign is more than one good evening. You take the Class 5 universe and plan twenty sessions: what the game remembers between them, how an evening is built, how standing opens and closes doors, and how the play styles take turns. Then you test it on a table, revise it safely, package it, and build the first act.

All eleven videos are also in one [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLFiLBlj5wNxc).

## What you need

- Artemis Cosmos 1.4.0, from Steam or itch.io.
- A Windows computer you can install a free editor on.
- About an hour a lecture, most of it your own typing.
