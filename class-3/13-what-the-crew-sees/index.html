    Class 3, Lecture 13 - What the crew sees¶

    What you will have at the end¶

    The same mission, read from the other side of the screen.

    For six lectures you have written records. Your crew never sees a record. They see a handheld with eight apps, and each app is made of your words: a heading here, a key there, a title after the words Go to. Some of those words you chose with care. Some you typed in a hurry as a name for yourself, and they are on the screen too.

    Today you go through the handheld one app at a time, find what reads badly in MyAway, and fix it. You also give the two crew members with nothing to do something to do.

    Dr Hale's handheld just after she beams down to the pad: the first screen is the list of eight apps.

    The screens on this page were played on the game itself. A few numbers come from the game's code and were not counted on a screen, and the page says so where they come up.

    You change four files: mission.amd and the three maps.

    The video¶

    About 6 minutes. Subtitles are available in the player.

    Before you start¶

    • MyAway as Lecture 12 left it. sbs lint MyAway says clean.
    • A command prompt open in C:\Cosmos\data\missions.

    Words for this lecture:

    Word Meaning
    Handheld What a console becomes when its crew member beams down
    App One row of the handheld's first screen: Crew, Act, Look, Pack, Tasks, Beam, Scan, Fire
    Bar The line that is always there: your name, your jobs, and where you are
    Covering Offered a choice that belongs to a job you do not have

    Step 1 - Read the screen¶

    Type nothing in this step. Read what your mission puts on the handheld today.

    The bar¶

    Three things, always there. This is Dr Hale in the first second on the ground:

    Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | pad

    Her name and her jobs come from your roster: the heading, and the Roles: line. The third word is where she is standing, and it is the name of a mark in your map file. Hold that thought until Step 2.

    When she is hurt the bar says so, and when she is down it says nothing else:

    Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | Kesh Relay - HP 2/3
Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | DOWN - needs a medkit

    The eight apps¶

    The handheld's first screen is a list: one full-width row for each app, its name and one line under it. They are the same in every mission:

    Crew | Who is out here, and the way home
Act | What you can do here
Look | What is within reach
Pack | What you are carrying
Tasks | What is asked of you
Beam | Where the ship can put you
Scan | Read the room you are in
Fire | Arm, then click the map

    Here is what each one lists, and which of your words it uses.

    App What it lists Your words in it
    Crew The ship, each person on the ground and where they stand, Everyone. For the one you pick: jobs, place, health, the last thing they said. Buttons: Report in, Beam up, Call and a name, Call for help The title on the card, roster names, Roles:, mark names
    Act The scene that is open: every line so far, then your choices Every % line and every choice
    Look Things in reach, as Use and a name, or Talk to and a name. Ways out, as Go to and an area's title. Then Further off: up to four things in sight. Then the last thing that happened to you Prop and person headings, area titles
    Pack What you carry, as an item and a count. Use medkit. Give to and a name, for somebody standing next to you Item keys
    Tasks A list: your own side story, then the party's quests, each a heading and a state. Under it, the page of the one you pick: its state, its objective, its text. Then Leads: what each quest points at and where Quest headings, Objective:, the text, Leads to:, area titles
    Beam To and an area's title, for each other area the ship can put you in. Never the one you are in: on the pad at the start it says No other site has a lock yet. Area titles
    Scan The place you stand, then everything in sight with its Scan: line Mark names, area titles, headings, Scan: lines
    Fire SAFE or ARMED, three settings, what the chosen one does, Arm or Make safe None

    Two of those numbers were read from the game's code and not counted on a screen: Look shows at most six things in reach and four further off, and Tasks shows at most four leads.

    A Further off row is a button, and pressing it walks you straight to that thing. The walk goes round walls, not round a hostile: a crew member pressed one in the yard and was downed walking through the sentry's square.

    Not every console has all eight. A console in a party of rooms, like Lecture 6's, has Crew and Act, and Tasks if its person holds a story. Look, Pack, Beam, Scan and Fire need a map to stand on.

    Act is a transcript¶

    Act does not show one room. It keeps everything this console has read, in order. Here is the end of Dr Hale's Act app after a talk with Marrow and two pickups. His long line is cut short here:

    Dr Ines Hale
[Ask what he has put by]
He nods at the scrub beside the landing pad. "Medical kit, under the netting. ..."
Dr Ines Hale picked up Medical kit.
Dr Ines Hale picked up Pump house key.
~ ~ ~
The conversation is over.
Dr Ines Hale picked up Toolbox (fuse x2).

    So a pickup is named by the prop's heading, the words the crew saw on the map, and the prop is gone from the map once it is picked up. When the heading does not say how many, the count follows in round brackets, in the pack's words: Toolbox (fuse x2). The pack itself says pump key x1 and fuse x2, the item's key. One thing, two names. That is Step 4.

    Who is offered what¶

    All of this was played with stand-in consoles on your Lecture 12 files.

    What you wrote Who gets it
    A choice with no if Everybody in the scene
    if medical The medic, when she is in the scene
    if medical, and nobody in the scene is a medic One person in the scene, with (covering for medical) after the words
    if skill science >= 3 Only somebody that good. Nobody covers for a skill
    if holding fuse >= 3 Only the one carrying them
    Needs: engineering on a prop Anybody else is told You would need engineering for that.
    For: medical on a side story Her Tasks app, and nobody else's. It is not on the ship's quest list

    The third row has a surprise in it. Covering is worked out scene by scene. Lt Ross walked up to Marrow alone, while Dr Hale stood on the landing pad six cells away. Ross was offered the medic's answer, last of the five:

    [Look at that cough (covering for medical)]

    Dr Hale was on the ground, and not beside him, so she was not in his scene. The answer is covered whenever the medic is not standing with Marrow.

    So a choice for a job is never out of reach because the right person is busy elsewhere. If only a medic's hands will do, ask for a skill, not a job.

    A scene opens for the one who clicked. With a prop's scene, a crew member standing within two cells is brought into it as well. That last sentence is from the game's code; the play showed it once, with two people at the terminal.

    Somebody with no job here¶

    Now look at the mission as Lt Ross, your helm officer. Her Tasks app:

    Party
Relight Kesh Relay
Active
Hold at the Relay
Done
State  Active
Light the beacon at Kesh Relay
The relay's beacon went dark nine days ago. Go down and light it.

    No story of her own. At Marrow she gets the answers everybody gets, and one she is covering. At the pump panel she is refused. Ens Vale at the terminal is the same: his science is 2, so the door code is not even offered. Two of your five crew members have come down to watch. That is Step 7.

    Step 2 - A mark is a word on the bar¶

    The bar said pad. That is the mark @pad in landing.tiles. Any mark a crew member can stand on is a word on their bar, in the Crew app beside their name, and at the top of Scan. An underscore is shown as a space.

    Now walk Dr Hale across your three maps, and read the bar on four more marks. A mark shows only while somebody stands ON its cell. The cell a person lands on when they walk into an area reads the area's title (The Dry Gully). One step further, onto the mark's own cell, and the bar reads the mark:

    Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | post
Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | yard
Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | arrive
Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | foot

    post and yard came with the starter, and they are places. arrive and foot are yours, from Lectures 8 and 9. You typed them as names for yourself, and they have been on screen ever since.

    Name a mark for the place, the way you would say it. Open ground\landing.tiles (press Text in the editor). Change two lines:

    entry: landing_pad

      P: pad @landing_pad

    In ground\gully.tiles:

    entry: gully_mouth

      a: dust @gully_mouth

    In ground\cistern.tiles:

    entry: foot_of_the_stairs

      f: floor @foot_of_the_stairs

    The gully names that last mark too, as the place its stairs come out. In ground\gully.tiles, under exits::

      to_cistern: cistern @foot_of_the_stairs

    Save the three files and run lint. It says clean. Played again, on the pad and one step into the gully, the bar reads:

    Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | landing pad
Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | gully mouth

    A mark never gives a hidden thing away. Two of your marks have something hidden on them: @scrub, where the medical kit lies until Marrow speaks of it, and @crate, where the spare cell waits for the tank to drain. The game does not show a mark like that. This is Dr Hale standing on the kit's cell before anybody has asked Marrow:

    Dr Ines Hale | medical, science | Kesh Relay

    The area's title, and nothing more. A cell with no mark at all reads the same way. Once Marrow has told of the kit, the same cell reads scrub, and stepping onto it picks the kit up: Act says Dr Ines Hale picked up Medical kit. and the cell is empty. So you may put a hidden thing on a mark. Name the mark for the place all the same, because it is on the bar from the moment the thing is found.

    Marks under a thing that blocks, like door and tent, are never stood on. Leave them.

    Step 3 - A title is read after "Go to"¶

    An area's title: is used in five places. Here are three of them, from your Lecture 12 files:

    Go to The Dry Gully
To The Dry Gully
! Sump crawler - somewhere not yet found

    The first is a button in Look and the second a button in Beam. Both put a word in front of your title, so a title that starts with The reads badly twice.

    The third is a lead in Tasks. Once the party has found the area, the lead ends with the area's title. The other two places are the bar and the top of Scan, when nobody is on a mark.

    In gully.tiles and cistern.tiles, change the two titles:

    title: Dry Gully

    title: Pump House Cistern

    Played again:

    Go to Dry Gully
To Dry Gully
! Sump crawler - Pump House Cistern

    Look only lists a way out to an area the party knows. Your gully is known: no, so until somebody has walked east there is no Go to button for it, and the trail sign is the only thing that says it is there. That is by design. Know that it is so.

    Step 4 - What you pick up, and what the pack calls it¶

    A prop you pick up is on screen under two names: its heading, and its Item: key.

    Where What it showed
    Look Use Brittle notice, Use Pump house door
    Further off ? Pump house key - 5 E
    Act Dr Ines Hale picked up Pump house key.
    Pack fuse x2, pump key x1
    At the door pump key fits
    Handing over Handed over medkit.

    Look, Further off and Act say the heading, Pump house key. The pack and the door say pump key, because that is the item's key with its underscore turned into a space. So she picks up a Pump house key and is carrying a pump key. To your crew those are two different things.

    Make the item's key the same words as the heading. Three lines name the pump house key, and all three must change together. In the Pump house key record:

    Item: pump_house_key

    In the Pump house door record:

    Opens with: key pump_house_key, check engineering 8, cut

    And in Marrow's scene:

    - [Show him the brass key](marrow_pump) if party pump_house_key

    The relay keycard has the same fault. In the Relay keycard record:

    Item: relay_keycard

    In the Relay door record:

    Opens with: key relay_keycard, check engineering 9, cut, signal relay_unlocked

    The note above the keycard record names the old key too. Change it to match.

    Name a pickup for what ends up in the pack. Until somebody picks it up, the heading is all the crew has: a ? Toolbox row under Look's Further off, and Toolbox in Scan, say where to walk and not what is there. And it is the heading Act says once they do. Change two headings:

    ### [Two fuses](toolbox)

    ### [Pim's medical kit](stash)

    Only the words in square brackets change. The keys stay, because other lines join to them.

    Save and run lint: clean. Played again:

    Dr Ines Hale picked up Pump house key.
Dr Ines Hale picked up Two fuses.
pump house key x1
pump house key fits
relay keycard fits

    When the heading and the item's key are the same words, Act and the pack name the thing the same way: Pump house key, pump house key x1. Two fuses and fuse x2 agree too, and because the heading already says how many, Act adds no count after it.

    Miss one of the three lines and lint tells you: nothing in the mission gives the old key any more. Step 10 has the warning.

    Step 5 - Scenery still gets scanned¶

    In Lecture 10 you learned that a prop with no words is scenery, and nothing offers to use it. Scan does not know that. This is the yard through Scan, on your Lecture 12 files:

    Kesh Relay
**Tent** - no reading
**Pump house key** - A brass key on a loop of wire, hung on a tent peg.
**Medical kit** - A sealed medical kit under camouflage netting.
**Old Marrow** - One human. Elevated pulse, and a wet cough.

    Scan lists everything in sight: scenery, pickups and people. The medical kit is on the list because Marrow had already told of it; before that it is not there at all. For each thing it shows the Scan: line. With no Scan: line it shows the description, and with neither it says no reading.

    A Scan: line does not stop a prop being scenery. Give your three pieces of scenery one each. In the Tent record, under Blocks: yes:

    Scan: Canvas, a camp bed, and a kettle that is still warm.

    In The pump record:

    Scan: A two stage pump. No power to it, and nothing wrong with it.

    In the Crate record:

    Scan: Empty. Somebody has been using it as a seat.

    Played again from the gully mouth:

    **Crate** - Empty. Somebody has been using it as a seat.
**Pim** - One human, young. Cold, and not hurt.

    A Scan: line is the cheapest clue you have. It costs the crew no walking. Use it to say what a thing is for before they cross the map to find out.

    Step 8 - How long is a line¶

    One length has been seen on the game: Look at that cough (covering for medical), 41 characters, fits on one line in Act. Past that, what can be measured is what the shipped missions do, so count yours against them.

    Counted The starter Yours, now Dawnline
    Choices 28 64 148
    A choice, middle length 19 characters 20 16
    Nine choices in ten are under 35 characters 35 29
    The longest choice 39 44 50
    A % line, middle length 27 words 27 words 23 words
    The longest % line 37 words 38 words 74 words
    A heading, middle length 11 characters 12 12

    Three things to plan with:

    • A choice that is covered grows by (covering for medical) and the like: up to 27 more characters on your longest job word. (From the game's code.)
    • The Act app shows the whole transcript and scrolls. A long line wraps onto more lines; it is never cut off. It is only read less.
    • In Act your choices are buttons at the end of the transcript, one under another, each as wide as its words.

    So keep to the starter's numbers until you have seen your own mission on a screen: a choice under 35 characters, a line under 40 words. Your longest choice is 44 characters, Fit two, and bridge the third clip with wire. It is on a prop that needs an engineer, so it is never covered. Leave it, and look at it first when you play.

    Step 9 - What the bridge sees¶

    Somebody stays aboard. Here is all of it.

    On the ship's tablet, the Boarding Party screen lists who has gone. This is Lt Ross, back aboard, with Dr Hale still down:

    Boarding Party  Kesh Relay
Going down to Kesh Relay
Lt Dana Ross  helm
In the party
Dr Ines Hale  medical, science
BEAM DOWN

    The ship's quest log shows the shared quests, and nothing of anybody's side story:

    Relight Kesh Relay
Active
Hold at the Relay
Done

    Pim's quest joins that list when an answer starts it, and not before.

    And Messages gets a line only when somebody on the ground presses Report in, in their Crew app:

    Dr Ines Hale
  Dr Ines Hale, reporting in.
* Sam
  The cat has opinions
* Mum
  Are you eating
* Mum
  Nan says hello

    The rows under it are the crew's mail from home. They come with the game, not with your mission. The reporting-in line is the only one of yours.

    That is everything. No scene line reaches the bridge. So the shared quests are the bridge's only window on the ground. Every time one starts or finishes, the bridge learns something. Lecture 16 builds a quest tree with that in mind.

    Give the bridge something to read¶

    Select a quest in that list and the Quest Log shows its description beside it, in full. The starter's is one sentence. For the people who never beam down, it is the whole briefing. Give it a page. In Relight Kesh Relay, replace the line under the closing --- with:

    ![](ship://starbase_civil) Kesh Relay, the only light on the Kesh run.

The relay's beacon went dark nine days ago. Go down and light it.

| What | What the ship knows |
|---|---|
| The beacon | Dark for nine days. No power in the lamp |
| The caretaker | Old Marrow. Not answering hails |
| The yard | A security frame on patrol. It answers to nobody now |

Every convoy on the Kesh run steers by that lamp. Until it is lit, they fly blind.

    You know every piece of it from Classes 1 and 2: a ship lead line, paragraphs, a table. starbase_civil is the hull story.mast gives the relay, so the picture is the station the ship is holding beside. The table says only what the ship could know from up there. Nothing in it gives away a scene.

    The side stories are a different matter. Their words under the fence are on one screen only, the page under the owner's Tasks list, on the ground. Nobody aboard reads them, so leave them short.

    Each row was tried on the finished files, one change at a time.

    The item renamed on the pickup, and Marrow's line still says if party pump_key item-nothing-gives, on the choice. Show him the brass key is offered to nobody
    Item: pump house key, with spaces item-nothing-gives, on the door and on Marrow's line. The pack shows pump house key x1, and it opens nothing

    Four of those, as lint printed them:

      [ERROR] line 12:8: entry 'pad' is neither a mark on this map nor x, y (tiles-entry)

      [WARNING] line 224:7: cache: no mark 'scrub' in landing - it is never placed (tiles-unknown-mark)

      [WARNING] line 22:3: exit to_cistern arrives at @foot, which is not a mark in cistern (tiles-exit)

      [WARNING] line 297: `Opens with: key pump_key` - nothing in this mission gives anybody `pump_key`: no pickup has `Item: pump_key`, nobody `Drops:` one and no answer says `; give pump_key`. So this door never opens with a key. Check the spelling against the `Item:` line of the thing you mean. Given in this mission: fuse, medkit, power_cell, pump_house_key, relay_keycard, tablet (item-nothing-gives)

    What lint cannot see¶

    None of these is a mistake lint could know about. They are choices of words.

    The words What the crew reads
    A mark named for yourself arrive, on the bar of anybody who stands there
    A title that starts with The Go to The Dry Gully
    An item key that is not the heading's words picked up Pump house key. in Act, then pump key x1 in the pack
    A choice too long to read at a glance Nobody has measured where it stops fitting

    After any rename, run lint, and then search the file for the old word. In VS Code, Ctrl+F, type the old key, and look at every line it finds.

    Step 11 - Play it¶

    sbs run server,helm,comms,science -m MyAway map=0

    This time do not play to win. Play to read.

    1. All three beam down. On each console, open every app on the list once and press Back.
    2. Read the bar. Walk off the pad and read it again.
    3. As Lt Ross, open Tasks, then talk to Marrow. Find her answer.
    4. As Ens Vale, walk to the terminal and find his answer. Every cell beside the terminal is within the sentry's notice, so it will come for him. Stun it first from the Fire app (a stun lasts about ten seconds), then read fast. Shots miss a lot, and no app tells you a shot hit or missed.
    5. As Dr Hale, pick up the brass key by the tent. Open Pack, then Act.
    6. Open Scan in the yard. Then walk east into the gully by the path behind the relay house, as in Lecture 9, and open Look. Press only the row you mean: a Further off row walks you there, sentry or no sentry.
    7. On a console left aboard (a fourth one, or one that has pressed Beam up), open the handheld's Boarding Party and Quests.

    Write down every word that makes you wince. That list is your exercise.

    If something goes wrong¶

    What you see Why What to do
    Lint says tiles-entry You renamed a mark and not the entry: line above it Make the two match
    Lint says tiles-exit The gully's exits: line still names the old mark in the cistern Step 2
    The brass key no longer opens the pump house The door's Opens with: still has the old item key Step 4. Lint says item-nothing-gives
    A story is Done and its owner never touched it Somebody else took the answer as covering Step 7. Use if skill if only one person may

    Exercise¶

    1. Stand on the cell where the medical kit is hidden, before and after you ask Marrow about it. Read the bar both times. The second time, read Act too: the step picked the kit up.
    2. Read every prop heading in your file after the word Use, and after the words picked up, then the same prop's line in the pack. Change the ones that jar, or that name one thing two ways.
    3. Read every Scan: line. Each should tell the crew something they could not see. Rewrite two.
    4. Find your three longest choices. Can each lose five words and keep its meaning?
    5. Break it and read lint: entry: pad; the mark on the h line renamed; Item: pump_key on the key only. Lint has a different thing to say about each.

    Then answer on paper, from the file alone:

    • Which words in your file are on the bar at some time? List the marks.
    • A party of Lt Ross and Ens Vale. Which answers are they covering, and for whom?
    • What does the bridge learn, and when, if nobody presses Report in?

    Checkpoint¶

    You are done when all five are true:

    • sbs lint MyAway says clean.
    • Every mark that can be stood on is named for its place.
    • The pack, the door and the heading use the same words for each key.
    • Every crew member on your roster has a story or an answer of their own.
    • You can say which of your words each of the eight apps shows.
